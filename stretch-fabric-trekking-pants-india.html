Finishes

C0 DWR plus anti-odour for trekking pants and softshell bottoms; moisture management plus anti-odour for leggings and next-to-skin pieces.

Durability

For trek and climbing pants we test abrasion to ISO 12947-2 (Martindale), with a typical target of 30,000+ rubs, and stretch and recovery to EN 14704-1 / ASTM D2594.
