03Sustainability

We promote eco-friendly products to global buyers

Sustainability is part of every sourcing brief we handle. With each enquiry we suggest lower-impact options wherever a mill can offer them, and we back every claim with documents you can check.

Recycled & renewable fibres

GRS recycled polyester and nylon, recycled insulation and fleece, lyocell, organic cotton and hemp blends.

Cleaner chemistry

PFC-free (C0) water repellency, OEKO-TEX tested fabrics and restricted-substance declarations from the mill.

Lower-impact processing

Solution-dyed yarns that skip conventional dyeing, and mills that invest in water recycling and renewable energy.

Proof, not slogans

Certificates and transaction certificates with each quotation, and traceability data for digital product passports.

Our rule: we never call a fabric sustainable unless the supplier can document it.

06Sourcing insights

Notes from the sourcing desk

Short pieces from our founder on what buyers and Indian mills need from each other. Full posts are on LinkedIn.

For buyers & mills

The mill you need exists. It just doesn’t have a website.

Search the open web for warp-knit tricot and you find East Asian mills with GSM, width, MOQ and test reports. Indian units in Surat and Ludhiana make the same cloth, but almost none publish it. India loses orders on missing evidence, not on skill.

Buying strategy

Do global buyers still need a sourcing agent?

Yes, but only a specialist. Buyers are spreading sourcing across countries while cutting supplier lists. They need someone who makes India less risky: the right mill for the spec, samples and test reports on time, and checks before shipment.

Market outlook

Outdoor technical fabrics, 2026 to 2036

PFAS-free water repellency becomes the minimum standard. Digital product records and recycling data enter every buying decision. Single-material, recyclable and bio-based fabrics move from trials to scale.

Product development

One puffer, three versions

A basic stand-collar puffer, a design-detail version and a hooded warmer one share the same core skills: quilting, fill and a down-proof shell. One Indian factory can develop all three for a buyer from a single programme.

08Questions

Questions buyers ask us

Are you a manufacturer?

No. Global Synctex Solutions is a sourcing agent. We work with carefully selected Indian mills and factories and manage the process for you, from shortlist to shipment.

What can you source from India?

Outdoor and performance fabrics and garments: shells, softshell, insulation, hiking, cycling, swim, resort wear and base layers. We also source hi-vis safety fabrics (EN 20471), coated and laminated fabrics including PUL and TPU laminates, suiting, shirting and medical uniform fabrics. See our outerwear & outdoor deck and workwear deck.

What minimum order quantities apply?

MOQs depend on the mill, the construction and the finish. Every option we send includes the mill’s MOQ, price and lead time so you can compare them side by side.

How do you check quality?

We agree test methods and pass levels before bulk, collect lab reports, and inspect at the factory against your approved sample and AQL level before shipment.

Do you offer eco-friendly and PFAS-free fabrics?

Yes. We promote eco-friendly options with every enquiry: GRS recycled polyester and nylon, recycled TPU membranes, PFC-free (C0) DWR, OEKO-TEX tested fabrics, lyocell and organic cotton. We share each supplier’s certificates with the quotation.

Do your partner mills have social compliance audits?

Many do, and we check before shortlisting. With each quotation we tell you which audits the mill holds (for example amfori BSCI, SMETA, WRAP or SA8000) and share the current report or certificate.

How do I start?

Send one fabric or product brief through the enquiry form, WhatsApp or email. We come back with Indian options to compare.
